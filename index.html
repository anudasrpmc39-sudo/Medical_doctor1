About Dr. Anupom Das

Professional profile, background, and clinical commitment.

Dr. Anupom Das is a medical doctor currently working in a government tertiary care hospital in Jashore, where he provides regular patient care in a busy public healthcare setting. In addition, he also consults patients in a private chamber during the afternoon and evening.

He is committed to compassionate, respectful, and evidence-based medical practice. His work includes diagnosis and management of adult medical conditions, follow-up of chronic diseases, interpretation of common investigations, prescription planning, and patient counseling.

Beyond clinical practice, he is also engaged in teaching undergraduate medical students, helping them build foundational knowledge, clinical reasoning, and patient-centered attitudes for future practice.
